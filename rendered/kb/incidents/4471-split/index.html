How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). Came in by app, email, or a booking-v2 form. The shared parser read every date month first; our tenants write the day first. Fixed.
  • hint: also a swap, but it came in through a booking-v3 form. That form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then read the value day first. People did what the hint said. The form is retired, so no fix is planned.
  • shift: the date moved by one day (Tuesday 12 May → Monday 11 May). No swap does that. These are the desk’s one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The reports point to time zones and DST. Still open.

The subject line tells you none of this. Read the body and look up the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run. Intake’s tag comes from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs that look alike. Merging by subject (0001) hid two of them.
  • Closing the big ticket does not close what was merged into it. Check each problem against the fix.
