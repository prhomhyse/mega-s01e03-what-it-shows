How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swapped (7 April → 4 July). Came in by app, email, or a booking-v2 form. The shared parser reads every date month first; our tenants write the day first.
  • hint: also a swap, but it came in through a booking-v3 form. That form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the value day first. People do what the hint says. Fix the hint, not the parser.
  • shift: the date moved by one day (Tuesday 12 May → Monday 11 May). No swap does that. These are the desk’s one-day moves (desk-notes.json). The reports point to time zones and DST.

The subject line tells you none of this. Read the body and look up the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run. Intake’s tag comes from the reporter’s browser and is often wrong.

Lessons

  • A big ticket can be several bugs that look alike. Merging by subject (0001) hid two of them.
  • Count once per report id: the ticket export repeats some reports.
